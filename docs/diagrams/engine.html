Nexa: three futures, one checkable score

No single prediction. Every forecast runs in three scenarios; everything they multiply is measured.

SLOW

45% of tasks with AI use today are done with AI by 2030 + 5% of tasks with no use yet routine skills lose value slowly

MODERATE (default)

75% of tasks with AI use today are done with AI by 2030 + 15% of tasks with no use yet most organisations adopt AI

FAST

95% of tasks with AI use today are done with AI by 2030 + 30% of tasks with no use yet agents reach new work

A role on top in all three → safe in all 3 futures. Measured: O*NET tasks × Anthropic Economic Index. Assumed (stated on /evidence): the three rates.

Role match score: four parts you can add up by hand

skills you have · up to 60
transferable · 15
future demand · up to 25
− barrier
weighted share of the role's skills you hold
missing skills you have a close neighbour for
role outlook in the chosen scenario (WEF, AI pull)
degree −8, licence −15 (0 if already in the job)
Age, gender and background are never inputs. RunPod semantic closeness is shown beside the score, never inside it.
